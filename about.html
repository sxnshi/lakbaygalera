ABOUT

Puerto Galera

Puerto Galera is a coastal town in Oriental Mindoro, known for its white-sand beaches, calm bays, and some of the best diving and snorkeling in the Philippines. Its name means “Port of Galleons,” after its naturally sheltered harbor. Surrounded by coral reefs, waterfalls, and green hills, it is also a UNESCO Man and Biosphere Reserve and home to Mangyan communities, making it a destination for both relaxation and adventure.

PROVINCE

Oriental Mindoro, Philippines

REGION
