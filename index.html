EXPLORE PUERTO GALERA

A haven in Oriental Mindoro known for its white-sand beaches, clear waters, and world-class diving. Whether you’re looking for a quiet island escape or an adventure, Lakbay Galera guides you to the best beaches, dive spots, and local experiences, along with practical tips for getting there and where to stay.

ABOUT

Lakbay Galera is a travel guide to Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro, covering its white-sand beaches, world-class dive spots, waterfalls, viewpoints, and local culture. “Lakbay” means journey, and this site is meant to be your starting point for exploring one of the Philippines’ most beloved island destinations.

OUR

PURPOSE

We created Lakbay Galera to help travelers plan their trips and to show off the beauty of Puerto Galera. From white-sand beaches to coral reefs and mountain springs, we want visitors to find useful, easy-to-read information and discover places they may not have known about.
